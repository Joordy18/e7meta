The [Modes page](#/modes) shows how often each hero shows up in a few of the game's modes. The numbers come from the game's own "Hero Success Rate Rankings", which we copy and keep week by week.

## What is covered

- **Hunts**: the five hunt bosses (Wyvern, Golem, Banshee, Azimanak and Caides) at three difficulties (Normal, Hard and Hell). That is fifteen rankings.
- **Abyss**: one ranking for every tenth floor, from floor 10 up to floor 120. Higher floors and lower floors can look very different, so pick the floor you are working on.
- **Expeditions**: the five expedition bosses (Pherus, Snag Lich, Symaqus, Gigantes and Moroi) at levels 1 to 3.

Each ranking lists a hero's share and its place in that list. The first row is gold. Use the dropdown to switch between bosses, floors and levels, and "Show more" to go past the first 25 heroes.

## What the percentage means

It is the figure the game publishes in its ranking, shown unchanged. We do not rescale it or combine it across modes, so compare heroes within one ranking rather than across two: a 50% in one boss is not the same thing as a 50% on another floor.

## Periods

Hunt rankings are weekly, and the Modes page shows the dates under the dropdown. The abyss ranking covers a longer stretch, and the game gives no dates for expeditions, so none are shown for them.

## Where a hero is used

Open any hero page and look for **Other game modes**. It lists the rankings that hero appears in, best first, with its rank in each. A hero that is in no ranking simply has no such block. To see what to wear on that hero, read [how to read the build stats](#/guides/how-to-read-the-build-stats).

## Limited-time modes: the Rift

The game also publishes a Rift season: its name, its dates and any restriction on the heroes you may bring. The Modes page shows it at the bottom, with a note saying whether the season has ended. At the time of our latest snapshot the last season was Nightmare of the Oceans, which ran from 2026-01-08 to 2026-08-27 and allowed only Earth heroes. The game published no hero ranking for it when we collected.

We only report what the game publishes. Everything else about a limited-time mode, such as how to clear it, is not in this data.
